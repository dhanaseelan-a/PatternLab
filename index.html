Known characters

D

Known middle characters

OPTIONAL

Example: 3??222766 is 9 characters, with middle 22 at positions 4–5. If your known text overlaps the prefix or suffix at a length, adjust the position or length range.

E

Unknown character types

Selected character groups apply to every ?. Known letters in fixed positions are always allowed.

F

Search patterns

? = unknown

Examples: 3???2766, 3?ab?2766. Edit any pattern to fix additional positions; configuration changes regenerate patterns.

G

Performance

No hard candidate limit. Fast mode minimizes display overhead; every candidate still goes through PDF authentication. Huge searches may run for days or longer.

H

Browser storage & privacy

OPTIONAL

No PDF is stored in the browser.

Saved PDFs are removed after password recovery succeeds or the search is exhausted. Stopping early keeps an opted-in PDF and checkpoint for later. Clearing browser site data can remove them at any time.
